The people, places, factions and events the corpus names — each tie carrying the sentence it was read from.

Read from the corpus's .codex files — spec §25's stand-off lore graph, 66 of them over the .lore prose, sharing one ontology that declares every category and every predicate. An entity is asserted once and amended by later books, so what you see folds every mention of a name; a tie asserted one way is read back the other, and those are marked. Nothing here is written for this site. Every quote is the corpus's own FROM evidence for the claim beside it, and every claim names the file it came from. scripts/codex_graph.py builds it.
