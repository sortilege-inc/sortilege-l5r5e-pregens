# Blood of the Lioness — pack audit

Pass A read: the nine records in full, all nine portraits, the adventure's three parts
(`l5r5e-0.4-blood-of-the-lioness.arc` / `.lore`), the pack's relationship map, and the
pencilled rationale. 2026-09-07. Decisions are numbered **D1…** for answering by number.

The adventure, in one paragraph, because half the proposals hang on it: in **1123** the PCs
are summoned to the **Akodo War College** in the **Castle of the Swift Sword** — by default as
Emerald Magistrates sent by acting Emerald Champion **Agasha Sumiko** — to research a
dispute over a **diamond mine** the Scorpion claim was promised them for aid at the Battle
of Snow Plain in 820. Magistrate **Kitsuki Kāgi** and the claimants **Shosuro Aishi**
(Scorpion) and **Ide Tsubame** (Unicorn) are delayed by rains, so the party has three days
in the **Military Library** (librarian **Kitsu Ayako**, peasant-born, haunted unknowing by
her ancestor **Kitsu Yū**'s shade), the **Ikoma Hall of Scribes** (bard **Ikoma Ichigo**;
historian Ikoma Hideri's account is missing), the parade grounds (drill sergeant **Akodo
Atsushi**), and an audience with the young daimyō **Kitsu Takeko** (advisor **Kitsu Yayoi**).
An offering at the ancestral shrine drops them into the **vision** of the battle (Part Two,
where they play the Advisors — out of scope here). In Part Three they gather testimony and
argue the mine before Kāgi.

## 2. Contradictions — flagged for a decision

*Applied 2026-09-07: D1, D3, D4, D5, D7. Ruled no change: D2, D9. Withdrawn: D6 (my misread,
see below). D8 ruled 2026-09-07: keep the mechanic — prose rewritten to the portrait (D47 applied); her yumi still open. Chiaki's lord field ruled: Ikoma Motonobu is her lord, Akodo Tsukune the garrison commander she is placed under — giri and Q7 say so. Open: D10 (with D41).*

**D1. Mirumoto Hisa serves a man who has been dead for three hundred years.** Her giri:
"serves **Akodo Heihachi**, commander of the war college's advanced students." Akodo
Heihachi is one of the adventure's **Advisors** — the 820 persona party (p.12). A present-day
teacher cannot report to him. Her `lord_name` is a different person again, Mirumoto Ton.
→ *Recommendation:* she answers to **Akodo Tsanuri**, the War College commander Umi already
serves; `lord_name` → Akodo Tsanuri. **Trade:** Mirumoto Ton (named nowhere else) goes; the
pack gains a lord two PCs share, and Heihachi is free to be the ancestor in Part Two.
*Alternative:* keep Ton as lord and only rename the commander.

**D2. Shinjo Yoshimoto's lord is a Crane.** *Ruled 2026-09-07: keep as is. Doji Hiroshige
is himself an Emerald Magistrate — middle management between Agasha Sumiko and the PCs.
Not a contradiction; the record stands.* `lord_name` **Doji Hiroshige**; his giri says
he "reports to the Emerald Champion in Otosan Uchi" as a Unicorn-nominated magistrate. In
this adventure the acting Emerald Champion is **Agasha Sumiko**, and by default she is who
sends the PCs. → *Recommendation:* lord → **Agasha Sumiko**. **Trade:** Hiroshige (named
nowhere else) goes; his giri — Champion's will vs Unicorn loyalty — becomes the adventure's
own *Choosing Sides* rule, which forfeits a Unicorn PC glory for siding against the clan.

**D3. Asako Shuichi's lord field and giri disagree, and the giri collides with Chiaki's
lord.** Field: **Asako Sakuko**. Giri and Q9: "serves **Shiba Tsukune**, the Phoenix's
archivist-diplomat to the Akodo War College." Chiaki's lord is **Akodo Tsukune**. Two
Tsukune in one party. → *Recommendation:* the archivist-diplomat is **Asako Sakuko** —
rewrite the two mentions, keep the field. **Trade:** one name change settles the mismatch
and the collision at once; a Loremaster serving an Asako is the likelier shape anyway.
*Alternative:* keep Shiba Tsukune and rename Chiaki's lord.

**D4. Ikoma Chiaki's mentor text is about her lord.** Mentor field: **Kuni Eichi** (Crab).
Mentor text: "**Akodo Tsukune** taught Chiaki that a commander owes her soldiers certainty
about their loyalty…" — Tsukune is her lord (giri, Q9). → *Recommendation:* keep Eichi and
rewrite the text for him; a Kuni — a Crab witch-hunter, whose craft is finding what hides
— is the better teacher of interrogation than a garrison commander:

> Kuni Eichi taught Chiaki that the guilty confess to end the silence and the innocent
> confess to end the doubt, and that the whole craft is telling which; she has cleared
> eleven men since and is not sure of one of them.

**Trade:** Tsukune stops doing two jobs; the mentor grant (Small Stature) is unaffected.

**D5. Utaku Seki's commander is a man; her lord is a woman.** `lord_name` **Utaku Tama**
(female). Q12 and Q15 say the Utaku commander "cannot find a way to tell **him**", "until
**he** prompts her". Either two people or a slip. → *Recommendation:* Tama **is** the
commander; two pronouns → her, and the giri names her. **Trade:** one fewer unnamed NPC.
*Alternative:* the commander is a second, male officer — then he needs a name.

**D6. Kakita Kensaku's death names the wrong clan.** Q20: "the way his mentor Ide Tokimochi
would have wanted—except the hand will belong to a Lion, **not a Crane**." Tokimochi is
Unicorn; Kensaku is the Crane. → *Recommendation:* "not a Kakita's". Minimal; the sentence
means his own family's hand.

**D7. Kitsu Kaede: paramount tenet Honor, honour 57, and she sells forbidden poisons to
Scorpion agents.** Q7: "brewing medicines the War College officially forbids — poisons
extracted from the same plants as the healers' antidotes — and trades them quietly to the
Scorpion agents … betraying the Lion every time she saves a Lion soldier's life." Her
disadvantage *Disdain for Duty and Loyalty* fits that; *Honor* as paramount does not.
→ *Recommendation:* paramount → **Compassion** (the soldier in front of her, at any cost —
which is exactly what her mentor taught her). No mechanical effect: attitude A's +10 honour
stands whichever tenet is paramount. **Trade:** the tenets agree with the conduct; honour
stays 57, which the GM can then read as a woman who has not been caught. *Alternative:*
keep Honor and soften Q7 to medicines rather than poisons — but that removes her engine.

**D8. Miya Ujitsuna's sheet says one usable hand; her prose says two hands with a tremor.**
Disadvantage: *Lost Arm or Lost Hand* — "You have only one usable hand or arm." Concept and
Q14: "hands move constantly … fingers drumming on her thigh, palms pressed flat against her
sides, one thumb working the opposite wrist." Q10/Q12: "cannot grip a brush steady",
"trembling from the palsy". **The portrait settles it**: two arms, the left hand gloved
black and resting on the map case, the right raised and gesturing. → *Recommendation:*
keep the mechanic, rewrite the prose to match the picture — a dead left hand in a glove,
a right hand that will not be still (full text under §7). **Trade:** the disadvantage becomes
visible on the page and matches canon art; if the palsy was the intent instead, that is an
upstream mechanics swap, and I only flag it.

**D9. Kakita Kensaku has *Meekness* ("extremely shy… cannot interrupt people of higher
status") and a portrait that is a swordsman with a bare blade raised.** Not necessarily
wrong — a smith sighting an edge is not asserting himself — but the picture reads
confident and the prose reads deferential. → *Flag only.* No change proposed unless you want
the first impression to acknowledge the gap ("stern at a blade, silent at a person").

**D10. Umi's giri is a desire and her ninjō repeats it, and both blur two different
deaths.** Giri: "must discover how the previous commander … died, because her lord … has
forbidden anyone from telling her" — a lord forbidding it cannot be the duty he sets.
Ninjō: "what her predecessor did in the winter of 820" — 820 is Snow Plain, three centuries
before her predecessor lived. → Reworked under §6.
